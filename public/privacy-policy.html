Salaam Haji ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and your choices.

  • Email address

b) Location Information

  • Approximate or precise device location (if you enable location features)

c) Ritual, Family, and App Usage Data

  • Enable account authentication and secure access

  • Improve reliability, performance, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Location Sharing and User Control

  • Location is only used for location-dependent features you choose to use.
  • Family location sharing can be turned on/off inside the app.
  • You can revoke location permission anytime from device settings.

Salaam Haji is intended for users of all ages, including children, and is designed to be family-safe.

We are committed to complying with applicable children’s privacy laws, including COPPA and other regional requirements where applicable. We do not knowingly collect, use, or share personal information from children except as necessary to provide core app functionality and as permitted by law.
